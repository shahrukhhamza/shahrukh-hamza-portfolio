04 — How I work

Most engineers build for the happy path. I build for when it breaks.

I've spent 3+ years shipping AI systems into real businesses. The lesson is always the same: the demo works — production is where the AI gets something wrong, two systems disagree, and an API stops responding.

That's the part I design for, so what I build keeps working long after launch day.

  1. 01

    Map the workflow

    Understand how the work happens today, which systems are involved and exactly where it breaks.

  2. 02

    Build & connect

    Integrate the systems and add AI only where it makes a real decision — not everywhere it could.

  3. 03

    Design for failure

    Plan for wrong AI outputs, API timeouts and conflicting data, with fallbacks, retries and alerts.

  4. 04

    Ship & hand over

    Deploy to production, document how it works and make sure your team can trust it.
