01 — About

What I actually do

I build AI-powered products and automation systems — the kind that take something manual, fragile, or disconnected and turn it into something that just works.

My work sits at the intersection of agentic AI, workflow automation, and full-stack engineering: connecting systems that were never designed to talk to each other, building AI agents that make decisions mid-workflow, and shipping the software around them so it's actually usable.

The part I care about most is what happens when things don't go as planned — when the AI is wrong, when two systems disagree, when the happy path breaks. That's usually where the real engineering is.
